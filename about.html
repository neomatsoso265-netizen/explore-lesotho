About Lesotho

Discover the Mountain Kingdom of Southern Africa.

Our Beautiful Country

Lesotho is a beautiful mountainous country located in Southern Africa. It is completely surrounded by South Africa and is known as the Mountain Kingdom because of its high mountains and breathtaking scenery.

The country offers visitors a peaceful environment, fresh mountain air, traditional villages and exciting outdoor activities.

Our Culture and Traditions

Traditional Clothing

The Basotho blanket is an important part of Lesotho's cultural identity. It is traditionally worn for warmth and during special occasions.

Traditional Music

Music and dance play an important role in Basotho celebrations, ceremonies and everyday community life.

Basotho Hospitality

Visitors can experience the kindness, friendliness and welcoming nature of Basotho communities.
